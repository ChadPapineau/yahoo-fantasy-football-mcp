# The plan — reading order

Ten documents, final after a three-round adversarial review (30 objections, 29 conceded and landed,
1 withdrawn on evidence, 0 pressed). Every document opens with a **decisions-at-a-glance** table
(decision · why · alternative considered · what would change it), carries a **Yahoo-dependency**
tag on line 5 so what survives a Yahoo denial is visible at a glance, and ends with its
**assumptions and unverified items by name**. Verification marks: `[V-…]` verified at the cited
source, `[A-n]` assumed, `[U]` unverified. **Nothing here is implemented**; the build starts after
the owner approves this package.

Read 01–06 (the structural plan) before 07–10 (the product plan); read the changelog's closing
summary if you want the review's outcome in one page.
